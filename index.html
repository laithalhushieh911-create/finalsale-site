Your rules, in plain switches

No scripts to write and no flowcharts. The settings read like the rules you already run your shop by, and every one of them can be flipped mid-live.

  • Final sale, enforced. Deny change-of-mind returns and post-auction cancel requests, with TikTok's own reason codes, so the decision holds up.

Questions sellers ask

When can I start?

FinalSale is a TikTok Shop developer app, and TikTok is reviewing it now. Join the list above and you'll get one email the day it clears, with a sign-in link. Connecting your shop takes about two minutes, and the first 24 hours run in review mode so you can watch before anything is sent.

Is this allowed under TikTok Shop's rules?

Yes. FinalSale works through the official TikTok Shop developer platform with permissions you grant explicitly, the same way other seller tools do. It applies TikTok's own policies: live-auction wins are final sale, so change-of-mind returns can be declined, while claims for damaged, defective, wrong, or missing items are always reviewed by you.

What if a buyer's item really did arrive broken?

Those claims are never auto-denied. You get a notification with the buyer's photos and note, the refund amount, and how much time is left. Approve with one tap, with or without asking for the item back, or deny. Optionally, you can let small claims under a dollar amount you choose be approved automatically.

Does it answer buyer messages from day one?

Returns and cancellations work the moment your shop is connected. Buyer-chat replies switch on as soon as TikTok approves the app's customer-service permission, which is in their review queue now; until then every message still shows up for you to answer from the app.

Do I need to install an app from the App Store?

No. FinalSale runs in your browser and installs to your Home Screen like any app: on iPhone, tap Share, then Add to Home Screen; on Android, use Install. Sign in there with the 6-digit code from your email and notifications work from then on. A native app is on the roadmap and will use the same account.

Will it reply to things it shouldn't?

It only answers a short list of routine intents: cancel asks, return questions, shipping status, ship time, and thank-yous. Anything with dispute or chargeback language, damage, anger, or that it isn't confident about is drafted and sent to you instead. Words you add to your escalation list always reach you. And for your first 24 hours, nothing is sent at all until you approve it.

What does it cost?

Nothing during beta. After launch it's $29 a month per shop, cancel anytime, and beta sellers keep a discount for as long as they stay. You'll be told by email before anything is charged.

Can I turn it off?

Any time. Pause stops all automation while still collecting requests for you. Disconnecting your shop stops everything and removes FinalSale's access; you can reconnect later.

Does it work for Whatnot or eBay Live?

Not yet. FinalSale is built for TikTok Shop first. Other live platforms are on the roadmap; tell us which one you sell on at hello@finalsale.app.
