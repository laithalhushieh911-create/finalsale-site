What happens while you're on a live

You set the rules once. Everything after that goes into one of three piles.

Handled for you

  • "Can I cancel? I didn't mean to bid"Final-sale reply; the cancel request is declined with TikTok's reason code.
  • Changed mind, wrong size, found it cheaper, "doesn't meet expectations"Denied inside TikTok's window with TikTok's own reason code, photos or not.
  • "Where's my order?", "when does it ship?", thank-yousReal tracking number, your handling time, your sign-off. Finished chats are marked resolved so your Resolution Rate climbs.
  • Delivered? A thank-you, in your voiceAbout two hours after the carrier marks it delivered: thanks for the support, a review if they love it, a message first if anything's wrong.
  • After delivery: a thank-you, and a nudge to reviewTwo hours after TikTok marks an order delivered, the buyer hears from you: thanks for the support, leave a review if you love it, message us first if anything's wrong. Your words or ours.

Comes to your phone

  • Damaged, defective, wrong or missing item, not receivedBuyer's photos, the order, the tracking and TikTok's clock. Approve or deny in one tap.
  • "It won't arrive in time"Comes to you with the shipment state: in transit, not shipped, or label with no pickup. Your call, never a bot's.
  • Chargeback or dispute language, upset buyersNothing is sent automatically; a drafted reply waits for you.
  • Deadline remindersHalfway through TikTok's window and again near the end, before it decides for you.

Never done automatically

  • Denying one of the five real claimsHard limit, not a setting.
  • Promising a refundReplies never commit money you haven't approved.
  • Anything in review mode or while pausedYour first 24 hours are drafts only; one switch stops everything.

Why the denials hold up

Auto-denying is the whole point, and the one thing that could hurt you if it's done wrong. Six safeguards keep it on the right side of TikTok's line.

  • Only non-claim reasons are ever auto-denied. Changed mind, wrong size, found it cheaper, ordered by mistake, "doesn't meet expectations." Each one gets the matching TikTok reason code, never a generic one.
  • The five real claims always go to a person. Damaged, defective, wrong item, missing items, not received. Photos alone don't change that: a "doesn't meet expectations" request with photos is denied under final sale with a note telling the buyer to refile as damaged or wrong item if that's what the photos show.
  • Anything it doesn't recognize goes to you. A reason code TikTok adds tomorrow isn't guessed at; it's sent to your phone like a claim.
  • It learns from TikTok's rulings. Every denial is followed up: upheld, or overturned on appeal. The reason codes that hold up win next time, across every FinalSale shop, and your upheld rate is on your Overview.
  • It checks that you shipped. A cancel request or a "won't get here in time" refund on an order you never handed to the carrier, past your handling time, is never auto-denied: TikTok sides with the buyer there. It comes to you marked "Not shipped yet" instead.
  • A circuit breaker. If TikTok overturns 15% of a shop's recent denials, FinalSale switches that shop to review mode on its own and tells you — before TikTok's 20% threshold, never after.

Every delivery ends with a thank-you

The moment that earns reviews is the moment the package lands, and it's the moment every seller is busy. FinalSale sends it for you, in your tone, with the buyer's name and what they bought.

  • Asks for a review the right way. "If you love it, a review helps." Never an incentive, never "leave 5 stars" — TikTok forbids paid or conditional reviews, so the wording stays clean.
  • Catches problems before they become claims. "If anything's not right, message us first" turns a would-be damaged-item return into a chat you can fix.

Questions sellers ask

When can I start?

FinalSale is a TikTok Shop developer app, and TikTok is reviewing it now. Join the list and you'll get one email the day it clears, with a sign-in link. Connecting your shop takes about two minutes, and the first 24 hours run in review mode so you can watch before anything is sent.

Is this allowed under TikTok Shop's rules?

Yes. FinalSale works through the official TikTok Shop developer platform with permissions you grant explicitly. It applies TikTok's own policies: since June 2026 TikTok treats LIVE Auction, collectible and pre-owned orders as final sale for change-of-mind returns, so those can be declined with TikTok's reason codes, while claims for damaged, defective, wrong, missing or not-received items are always reviewed by you. Every decision is one TikTok lets a seller make by hand in Seller Center; FinalSale just makes it on time.

Which return requests does it deny?

Change-of-mind ones: changed mind, wrong size, found it cheaper, ordered by mistake, "doesn't meet expectations" — with TikTok's own reason code. Everything that needs a judgment comes to you with the information ready: damaged, defective, wrong item, missing items, not received, and "it won't arrive in time" (with the shipment state). Those reach your phone with the buyer's photos. A "doesn't meet expectations" request gets TikTok's "lack of evidence" reason when there are no photos, and a final-sale denial with a note to refile as damaged or wrong item when there are. Anything with a reason code FinalSale doesn't recognize goes to you too.

What if TikTok starts overturning my denials?

FinalSale follows up on every denial to see what TikTok did with it. If 15% of your recent denials are overturned, it switches your shop to review mode by itself and notifies you, so you're never near TikTok's 20% Invalid Seller Rejection threshold. Your upheld rate is on your Overview at all times.

Does it ask buyers for reviews?

Yes, once per order, about two hours after the carrier marks it delivered: a thank-you in your tone with the buyer's name and item, "a review helps if you love it," and "message us first if anything's wrong." It never offers anything in exchange for a review (TikTok forbids that), never sends while a return or cancel request is open on the order, and you can reword it or switch it off in Settings.

Will it reply to things it shouldn't?

It only answers a short list of routine intents: cancel asks, return questions, shipping status, ship time, and thank-yous. Dispute or chargeback language, damage, anger, or anything it isn't confident about is drafted and sent to you instead. Words you add to your escalation list always reach you. And for your first 24 hours, nothing is sent until you approve it.

Do I need to install an app from the App Store?

No. FinalSale runs in your browser and installs to your Home Screen like any app: on iPhone, tap Share, then Add to Home Screen; on Android, use Install. Notifications work from then on.

What does it cost, and what is an AI credit?

Nothing during beta, no card needed. After launch: Basic $99, Pro $299, Expert $799 a month, or 20% less billed annually. An AI credit is one AI-written reply to a buyer; routine messages use your stored replies and cost nothing. Run out and stored replies take over; top up in a tap. Cancel anytime.

Can I turn it off?

Any time. Pause stops all automation while still collecting requests for you. Disconnecting your shop stops everything and removes FinalSale's access; you can reconnect later.
