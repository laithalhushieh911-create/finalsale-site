Questions sellers ask

Is this allowed under TikTok Shop's rules?

Yes. FinalSale works through the official TikTok Shop developer platform with permissions you grant explicitly, the same way other seller tools do. It applies TikTok's own policies: live-auction wins are final sale, so change-of-mind returns can be declined, while claims for damaged, defective, wrong, or missing items are always reviewed by you. The app is currently going through TikTok's review process for developer apps.

What if a buyer's item really did arrive broken?

Those claims are never auto-denied. You get a notification with the buyer's photos and note, the refund amount, and how much time is left. Approve with one tap, with or without asking for the item back, or deny. Optionally, you can let small claims under a dollar amount you choose be approved automatically.

Do I need to install an app from the App Store?

No. FinalSale runs in your browser and installs to your Home Screen like any app: on iPhone, tap Share, then Add to Home Screen; on Android, use Install. Notifications work from there. A native app is on the roadmap and will use the same account.

Will it reply to things it shouldn't?

It only answers a short list of routine intents: cancel asks, return questions, shipping status, ship time, and thank-yous. Anything with dispute or chargeback language, damage, anger, or that it isn't confident about is drafted and sent to you instead. Words you add to your escalation list always reach you. And for your first 24 hours, nothing is sent at all until you approve it.

Can I turn it off?

Any time. Pause stops all automation while still collecting requests for you. Disconnecting your shop stops everything and removes FinalSale's access; you can reconnect later.

Does it work for Whatnot or eBay Live?

Not yet. FinalSale is built for TikTok Shop first. Other live platforms are on the roadmap; tell us which one you sell on at hello@finalsale.app.
