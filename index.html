You set the rules once. Everything after that is sorted into three piles.

TikTok grades you on exactly this

Your Shop Performance Score (0–5) decides how much traffic you get, whether you can run auctions, whether you keep affiliates, and how fast you get paid. Three of its six metrics are customer service. Here is what TikTok's own seller policies say, and what FinalSale does about each.

Customer-service rule

24-Hour Response Rate: at least 90%

Every buyer chat needs a meaningful reply within 24 hours — “Hi” doesn't count, weekends count, and TikTok's own chatbot replies are excluded. Below 90% is a warning; below 85%, enforcement.

With FinalSale: routine questions get a real answer in seconds, at 2am and on Sundays, sent from your shop's chat as you — not from TikTok's canned chatbot.

Shop Performance Score metric

IM Dissatisfaction Rate

The share of your chats rated one or two stars over 60 days. TikTok's own estimate: every hour a question sits unanswered raises one- and two-star ratings by about 5%. Top sellers stay under 15%.

With FinalSale: the wait is gone for routine questions, and the answer is accurate — the real tracking number, your real handling time. Anything heated goes to a person, never a bot.

Shop Performance Score metric

After-Sales Handling Time: 12 hours or less

Your average time to handle refund, return, cancellation and replacement requests over 60 days. TikTok's current guidance: 12 hours or shorter. Miss the hard deadlines — 24 hours on cancellations and unshipped refunds, 48 hours on product-issue returns, 2–4 business days on the rest — and TikTok may approve the request for you.

With FinalSale: return and cancel requests are denied in seconds; the few that need you (damaged, defective, wrong, missing, not received) reach your phone within a minute with a countdown. Your average drops from hours to minutes, and nothing is ever approved by default.

After-sales rule

Invalid Seller Rejection Rate: under 20%

Measured over a rolling 8 weeks. At 20% or higher you get a warning, and TikTok moves your after-sales requests to mandated platform review — it decides your returns for you.

With FinalSale: every rejection uses TikTok's own reason codes for that request, and damaged, wrong or missing-item claims are never auto-denied, so your rejections hold up.

LIVE Auction rules, 2026

Auction wins are binding and final sale — the asks didn't stop

Since June 2026, change-of-mind returns aren't allowed on LIVE Auction, collectible and pre-owned orders. But buyers can't cancel an auction order themselves — every request lands on you with a 24-hour clock — they keep 14 days to claim damage or “doesn't match the stream,” and TikTok warns that “unjustified rejection or delay may trigger automatic refunds and harm shop performance.”

With FinalSale: the “can I cancel” chats, the pre-shipment cancel requests, and the “doesn't meet expectations” claims that replaced change-of-mind returns are each answered the right way, inside the window, with a reason TikTok accepts.

Account Health Rating

Response failures cost points you can't afford

Every shop starts at 200 points. Customer-service violations can deduct points. At 150, no new listings or campaigns for 7 days; at 100, 14 days; at 50, 28 days; at 0 the shop may be permanently deactivated.

With FinalSale: every action is one a seller may take by hand in Seller Center, with TikTok's reason codes, logged with the reason — nothing that adds a violation, and a record if you ever need to appeal.

Your rules, in plain switches

No scripts to write and no flowcharts. The settings read like the rules you already run your shop by, and every one of them can be flipped mid-live.

  • Deny by default. Every return and post-auction cancel request is denied with TikTok's own reason code — except the five only you can judge: damaged, defective, wrong item, missing items, not received.
  • It learns. Every denial is followed up: did TikTok keep it, or overturn it on appeal? The reasons that hold up win next time, across every FinalSale shop, and you see your upheld rate in Activity.

  • Review mode first. For the first 24 hours nothing goes out without your tap, so you can see exactly how it would have replied.

Questions sellers ask

When can I start?

FinalSale is a TikTok Shop developer app, and TikTok is reviewing it now. Join the list above and you'll get one email the day it clears, with a sign-in link. Connecting your shop takes about two minutes, and the first 24 hours run in review mode so you can watch before anything is sent.

Is this allowed under TikTok Shop's rules?

Yes. FinalSale works through the official TikTok Shop developer platform with permissions you grant explicitly, the same way other seller tools do. It applies TikTok's own policies: since June 2026 TikTok treats LIVE Auction, collectible and pre-owned orders as final sale for change-of-mind returns, so those can be declined with TikTok's reason codes, while claims for damaged, defective, wrong, or missing items are always reviewed by you. Every decision is one TikTok lets a seller make by hand in Seller Center; FinalSale just makes it on time.

Which return requests does it deny?

All of them, with TikTok's own reason code, except the five only you can judge: damaged, defective, wrong item, missing items, and not received. Those reach your phone with the buyer's photos. Everything else is denied whether or not photos are attached: a "doesn't meet expectations" request gets TikTok's "lack of evidence" reason when there are none, and a final-sale denial with a note to refile as damaged or wrong item when there are. Every denial is logged with its reason.

Does it get better over time?

Yes. FinalSale follows up on every denial to see what TikTok did with it: kept it, or overturned it after a buyer appeal. Those outcomes, across every shop on FinalSale, decide which of TikTok's reject reasons it uses for each kind of request, so the upheld rate climbs as the network grows. Your own upheld rate is on the Activity screen.

What if a buyer's item really did arrive broken?

Those claims are never auto-denied. You get a notification with the buyer's photos and note, the refund amount, and how much time is left. Approve with one tap, with or without asking for the item back, or deny. Optionally, you can let small claims under a dollar amount you choose be approved automatically.

Does it answer buyer messages from day one?

Returns and cancellations work the moment your shop is connected. Buyer-chat replies switch on as soon as TikTok approves the app's customer-service permission, which is in their review queue now; until then every message still shows up for you to answer from the app.

Do I need to install an app from the App Store?

No. FinalSale runs in your browser and installs to your Home Screen like any app: on iPhone, tap Share, then Add to Home Screen; on Android, use Install. Sign in there with the 6-digit code from your email and notifications work from then on. A native app is on the roadmap and will use the same account.

Will it reply to things it shouldn't?

It only answers a short list of routine intents: cancel asks, return questions, shipping status, ship time, and thank-yous. Anything with dispute or chargeback language, damage, anger, or that it isn't confident about is drafted and sent to you instead. Words you add to your escalation list always reach you. And for your first 24 hours, nothing is sent at all until you approve it.

What does it cost?

Nothing during beta, no card needed. After launch there are three plans sized by order volume: Basic at $99, Pro at $299 and Expert at $799 a month, each with a monthly allowance of AI credits and unlimited return decisions. New accounts get the first 7 days free; beta sellers keep 30% off for 12 months. Cancel anytime, and you'll be told by email before anything is charged.

What is an AI credit?

One AI-written reply to a buyer. Routine messages (cancel asks, "where's my order", shipping times, thank-yous) use your stored replies and cost nothing. The rest go to the AI and use one credit each. If you run out, stored replies take over so nothing goes unanswered, and you can top up in a tap.

Can I turn it off?

Any time. Pause stops all automation while still collecting requests for you. Disconnecting your shop stops everything and removes FinalSale's access; you can reconnect later.

Does it work for Whatnot or eBay Live?

Not yet. FinalSale is built for TikTok Shop first. Other live platforms are on the roadmap; tell us which one you sell on at hello@finalsale.app.
